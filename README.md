# Mubasshir Sayyed — Personal Portfolio v2

## Quick setup
Open `index.html` in a browser. No Node.js is required.

## IMPORTANT: changing your resume
You do **not** need to edit HTML every time your resume changes.
Simply replace:
`assets/resume.pdf`
with your newest PDF and keep the exact filename `resume.pdf`.

The Resume buttons automatically open the current file.

## Changing your photo
Replace `assets/profile.png` with your new photo and keep the filename `profile.png`.

## Personal links
Your GitHub and LinkedIn are already added. Email and phone are taken from the supplied resume.

## Files
- index.html — website structure/content
- style.css — complete UI
- script.js — small interaction
- assets/profile.png — profile image
- assets/resume.pdf — current resume
